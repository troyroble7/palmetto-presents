Channel Program

How Palmetto LightReach Measures EPC Partners

The Palmetto LightReach Channel Program scores every EPC partner on six KPIs. Tiers are earned — not given. Platinum is reserved for EPCs who perform in the top tier on all metrics that matter to capital markets, customer experience, and operational delivery.

KPI 1Install Quality (First-time M1/M2 pass rate)
KPI 2Aging Backlog (>60 & >115 day PTO aging)
KPI 3Customer NPS (Net Promoter Score)
KPI 4Collection Rate (Current payment %)
KPI 5Tranche Execution (On-time tranche fulfillment)
KPI 6Monthly Volume (Active NTPs relative to channel)

The Plan

Closing the Gap — Three Moves to Full Platinum

Move #1: M1 First-Pass +1.1 points

Close the 73.9% → 75% gap. Target a handful of top-cited M1 missing-flag categories. PLR ops team has the data — this is a weekly cadence item with Tim Hetterman.

Move #2: Aging >60 days 30% → 18%

Dedicated 2-week sweep of the 154 open projects. Coordinated push with utility partners on pending PTO submissions. This single move unlocks the Platinum floor.

Move #3: NPS 2 → 10+

Weekly customer update cadence + proactive 30/60/90-day status calls. Pair with cancel-rate reduction via sales rep expectation-setting. Dan Devito's training resources available.

The Upside

Hit all three and SpartanX becomes the reference EPC for the entire PLR channel — Platinum on all six KPIs, not just Volume and Quality.
