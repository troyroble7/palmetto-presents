Why NPS Matters

The Customer Voice Is the Business

Net Promoter Score measures how likely a customer is to recommend PLR + SpartanX to a friend. Scores range from -100 to +100. The scale:

>15Platinum
>10Elite
>5Pro
>0Certified

A higher NPS correlates with lower cancel rate, lower service complaints, higher referrals, and better collection performance. It is a leading indicator of portfolio health.

Where Customer Friction Shows Up

Friction Areas

What Customers Tell Us

Timeline Expectations

Customers who saw long gaps between milestones (sales → install → PTO) rate lower. Install Approved → PTO at 46-62 days is a fixable expectation gap.

Communication Frequency

Detractor patterns show customers going weeks without updates during the Install → PTO phase. Proactive status updates are the highest-leverage fix.

Cancel Rate Correlation

Cancel rate at 67% is strongly linked to CX friction — customers who cancel rarely report a strong experience. Reducing cancel rate directly lifts NPS.

Promoter Patterns

Customers who get weekly updates + proactive communication report 5-star experiences — even when timelines are long. Communication is the controllable lever.
