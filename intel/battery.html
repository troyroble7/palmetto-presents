Battery Sector Intelligence

Updated April 17, 2026  |  Palmetto LightReach Competitive Intelligence
Bottom line: Battery attachment going mainstream. 38% national (Ohm Q3-25), HI 92%, CA NEM3 87%. PLR offers both integrated solar+battery and standalone Energy Backup lease. Category is the single biggest growth vector in residential solar.

Market Data (Ohm Q3 2025)

  • National attachment: 38%
  • Hawaii: 92% (NEM 3.0 drove attachment)
  • California NEM 3.0: 87%
  • California overall: 79%
  • Illinois: 48%
  • Arizona: 46%
  • Q3-25 deployed: 1,026 MWh (+40% YoY)
  • Storage pricing: $1,074/kWh median H2 2025 (+3.6% YoY)

PLR Battery Products

Integrated Solar + Battery

  • 25-year TPO lease includes battery replacement at $0 cost to homeowner
  • Supported OEMs: Tesla PW3, Enphase IQ 10C, SolarEdge Home Battery
  • LONGi + PW3 crown jewel combo — only 60 EPCs across PLR channel approved

Standalone Energy Backup Lease (EB)

  • Battery-only lease product — no solar required
  • 675 FICO minimum
  • 10/12-year terms
  • $0 upfront
  • Active in 18 states
  • $75K max project
  • No PPL competitor offers battery-only
  • Tesla PW3, Enphase 10C, SolarEdge Home Battery (Franklin aPower2 on pause Mar 2026)

VPP / Grid Services Landscape

  • Sunrun GridRewards: 106K+ VPP customers, 18 programs, 4 GWh networked, 425 MW peak dispatch. Target 10 GWh by EOY 2028. Sunrun’s one genuine moat.
  • GoodLeap GoodGrid (GEMS): Launched 2024. $40/mo rewards. 1.5 GW target over 5 years. Reliant/NRG (TX), CT Green Bank, CA DSGS
  • Tesla Electric + Tesla VPP: Tesla PW3 owners auto-enrolled, 1M+ cumulative Powerwalls, 25% in VPP
  • SOLRITE (TX, Feb 2026): First-ever battery-only VPP in Texas. sonnen hardware. Target 600 MWh by EOY 2026
  • PLR strategy: Grid+ TX REP battery bonus ($4/kWh/mo) + Energy Backup product. Simpler, cleaner value prop than VPP aggregation

Battery Tariff Landscape (Updated Mar 2026)

  • Chinese LFP batteries: ~82% combined import duties (Section 301 + reciprocal)
  • LG Energy Solution Qcells partnership (Feb 2026): 5 GWh utility-scale BESS (LFP, MI/AZ, 2028-2030)
  • Tesla Megapack 3: volume production H2 2026 at Houston Megafactory (50 GWh/yr), 15 GWh multi-billion dollar contract signed
  • Enphase ADLP price increase: 10-20% on select microinverters effective Feb 9, 2026

PLR Competitive Position

  • 25-year battery replacement coverage is a unique protection — no competitor matches
  • LONGi + PW3 approved combo gives top-tier PLR EPCs (SpartanX among them) access to the most desirable equipment pairing in residential solar
