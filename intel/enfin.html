EnFin / Qcells

EnFin Competitive Intelligence

Updated April 17, 2026  |  Palmetto LightReach Competitive Intelligence
Bottom line: Qcells’ financing subsidiary. Hybrid loan + TPO. Debut TPO ABS (CLS 2026-1) priced April 7, 2026 at +250 bps, 25 bps wider than PLR’s Feb 27 Sabal 2026-1 (225 bps). Vertically integrated with Qcells manufacturing, but limited EPC footprint (8 states + PR). Qcells New Homes launch (Mar 2026) is the builder-channel signal to watch.

Company Overview

  • Full name: EnFin, LLC (wholly owned subsidiary of Hanwha Qcells)
  • Parent: Hanwha Qcells (subsidiary of Hanwha Solutions, KOSPI-listed)
  • HQ: Dalton, GA (Qcells US HQ)
  • Founded: 2021; began originating residential solar loans Oct 2022; officially launched Jan 2023
  • TPO launch: January 2024
  • Cumulative contracts: 22,000+ (as of Nov 2024). $1B+ total contract value
  • Key advantage: Vertically integrated with Qcells modules + financing
  • CEO: Alex Kaplan (ex-Mosaic/SolarCity/SunFi)
  • Scott Moskowitz (Qcells) elected SEIA Board Chair 2026 (Jan 23, 2026) — political influence

Qcells New Homes Launch (Mar 31, 2026) — Competitive Signal

Watch closely: Qcells launched Qcells New Homes — a dedicated division for residential homebuilders. Direct-from-manufacturer, turnkey solar + storage platform with operating leases via EnFin. Lease designed to not impact homebuyer DTI — key selling point for builders. Direct competitor to PLR’s LightReach New Homes.
  • Sources: AltEnergyMag (Mar 31), Solar Power World (Mar 31)
  • DTI-neutral product is a real competitive threat in the builder channel
  • Vertical integration advantage: Qcells modules + EnFin lease + dedicated builder support

Supply Chain — Qcells Manufacturing Footprint

  • UFLPA RESOLVED Mar 6, 2026: Qcells GA production resumed. All 1,000 furloughed workers returned. 4-month disruption (Nov 2025 – Mar 2026) behind them
  • Cartersville, GA: $2.5B expansion, $1.45B DOE loan (Dec 2024). First fully integrated US silicon solar value chain (ingot-to-module). 3.3 GW annual capacity
  • Combined Dalton + Cartersville: 8.4 GW US module capacity. Target ~4,000 employees by EOY 2026
  • AD/CVD tailwind (Feb 2026): Qcells co-petitioner. Preliminary CVD: 126% India, 104% Indonesia, 81% Laos. AD decision Apr 21, 2026. Final CVD Jul 6, 2026
  • LG Energy Solution partnership (Feb 2026): 5 GWh utility-scale BESS deal (LFP, MI/AZ, 2028-2030)
  • EcoRecycle (Jun 2025): First C-Si producer with full value chain + recycling

EPC Payment Issues (Summer 2025)

  • EnFin had problems paying EPCs on time
  • Repriced EPC payouts negatively
  • Still owes many EPCs
  • Reputational damage with installer partners not yet overcome

Customer Complaints

  • EnergySage reviews cite undisclosed acceleration clauses
  • Buyout confusion (told anytime, actually 5 years)
  • Lease terms buried in fine print

Vulnerabilities

  • Loan-based ABS exposure — same asset class as GoodLeap’s deteriorating loan book. Subject to same KBRA solar loan ABS index headwinds (late-stage delinquencies at all-time high)
  • TPO ABS debut priced wider than PLR — CLS 2026-1 at +250 bps vs PLR Sabal 2026-1 at +225 bps (25 bps gap)
  • RBC sole structurer — single counterparty risk (same vulnerability as GoodLeap/Apollo)
  • Limited TPO history — only 15 months of origination
  • Qcells module dependency — vertical integration is a strength for Qcells-panel EPCs but limits EPCs using other manufacturers
  • Small TPO footprint — 6 states vs PLR’s 31 + PR
  • EPC payment issues (Summer 2025) — reputational damage with installer partners
  • No media platform, no cross-sell ecosystem, no SFR/rental product

Strengths to Watch

  • Hanwha/Qcells backing — deep pockets, publicly traded parent, manufacturing scale
  • Vertical integration — module + financing in one package is compelling for Qcells EPCs
  • Growing fast — 22,000 contracts in ~2 years, $1B+ contract value
  • KBRA ratings affirmed on loan ABS — no downgrades (unlike GoodLeap)
  • Green ABS framework — ESG positioning
  • Qcells New Homes (Mar 2026) — purpose-built builder division with EnFin lease. DTI-neutral = real threat to PLR New Homes
