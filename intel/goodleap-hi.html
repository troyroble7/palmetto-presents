GoodLeap Hawaii Pricing Update

Updated April 17, 2026  |  Palmetto LightReach Competitive Intelligence
Bottom line: GoodLeap price increase in HI (−$0.75/W effective Mar 4, 2026) makes PLR the clearly superior choice. HI battery attachment is 92% (highest in US). Rising Sun Solar is the dominant installer. Troy and his wife own a 77 kW PLR system in Kihei, Maui.

HI Market Context

  • Battery attachment: 92% (highest in US) — NEM 3.0 equivalent forced solar+storage bundling years ago
  • Utility: Hawaiian Electric (HECO, MECO, HELCO) across islands
  • 100% renewable goal: State mandate by 2045
  • Hawaii went 100% battery requirement in 2015 — teaches the rest of the US what’s coming

GoodLeap HI Price Increase (Mar 4, 2026)

  • EPC payout cut: −$0.75/W in Hawaii
  • Second nationwide price increase in 3 weeks (first was Feb 10, 2026)
  • Part of GoodLeap’s pricing death spiral — raising prices to compensate for poor collections
  • Makes PLR’s HI value proposition even more competitive
