GoodLeap Competitive Intelligence

Updated April 17, 2026  |  Palmetto LightReach Competitive Intelligence
Bottom line: Loan company trying to become a TPO company while legacy loan book burns. All 3 rating agencies downgrading. FBI investigation active. MN AG and VA AG lawsuits. FL/TX retreat. Two nationwide TPO price increases in 3 weeks.

Company Overview

  • Full name: GoodLeap, LLC (formerly Loanpal; originally Paramount Equity Mortgage)
  • CEO: Hayes Barnard (Founder, Chairman, CEO)
  • HQ: Roseville, CA & New York
  • Employees: ~1,235 (declining). Glassdoor 3.2/5 (down 12% YoY), 45% recommend, 33% positive outlook
  • Revenue: ~$500M (Dec 2024)
  • Valuation: ~$17.7B (secondary market; likely inflated — no equity raise since Oct 2021 at $12B)
  • Total financed: $32B+ to 1M+ homeowners across 50 states since 2018
  • TPO launch: June 2024 (only 22 months old vs PLR’s multi-year platform)
  • Narrative pivot: Hayes pushing “sustainable home upgrades” positioning ($450B TAM claim)

Legacy Loan ABS — Rating Agency Downgrades (All Three)

KBRA (Mar 19, 2026) — Latest and Worst

  • 18 classes DOWNGRADED + 35 classes affirmed across 18 securitizations spanning GoodLeap / LoanPal / Mill City shelves
  • KBRA cited “ongoing deterioration in collateral credit performance” and “decrease in overcollateralization since last review”
  • Downgrades to CCC(sf) and below reflect “current or imminent and likely prolonged deferral of interest and increased risk of principal loss”
  • 64% escalation from May 2025 action (18 vs 11 downgraded classes)

S&P Global (Sep 30, 2025)

  • 13 of 16 ratings downgraded across 6 GoodLeap trusts
  • Class A notes on multiple trusts dropped to BBB−
  • Class C notes on 2022-2, 2022-3, 2022-4 undercollateralized
  • Negative excess spread: −1.82% to −3.44%. Cumulative default rates: 5.99% to 7.10%, accelerating

Fitch (Jun 6, 2025 + Feb 17, 2026)

  • Downgraded Class B and C notes across GoodLeap trusts
  • Feb 2026: “Non-Prime Loans and State Concentrations Drive U.S. Solar ABS Loan Defaults” — validates PLR’s 670+ FICO floor

KBRA Solar Loan ABS Index — All-Time Worst

  • Jan 2026: Early-stage and late-stage delinquencies at ALL-TIME HIGH since index inception. Net losses +173 bps YoY.
  • Feb 2026: Late-stage delinquencies up 12 bps to 2.13%. Net losses up 122 bps YoY.
  • PLR’s TPO ABS is NOT in this index — different asset class

  • 17-state AG Pink Energy probe — expanding, not winding down
