2026 Solar Market Overview

Updated April 17, 2026  |  Palmetto LightReach Competitive Intelligence
Bottom line: 2026 is a trough year in MW (-18-26% YoY) but TPO market share jumps from 45% to 69-70% as 25D residential ITC expires. The market is consolidating around fewer, higher-quality issuers with capital markets access. PLR is positioned as the pure-play TPO with the strongest financial foundation.

2026 Core Forecast (SEIA / Wood Mac / Ohm / EnergySage)

  • 2025 full year: ~4.3–4.7 GW
  • 2026 = trough year: 3.5 GW (-18-26% YoY). Market bottoms then recovers. 86 GW total US new capacity (SEIA)
  • TPO share: 69-70% in 2026 (up from 45% in 2025) — SEIA/Wood Mac/Ohm all confirm
  • Barclays: TPO share could reach 90%+
  • Residential pricing: $2.49/W median H2 2025 (+0.4%)
  • Storage: $1,074/kWh (+3.6%)
  • Storage attachment: 38% national Q3-25, CA 79%, CA NEM3 87%, HI 92%
  • Utility rates: +7.4% YoY nationally. Data center demand tripling electricity needs by 2030

Policy Context (OBBBA)

  • Signed Jul 4, 2025
  • 25D (homeowner ITC) EXPIRED Dec 31, 2025
  • 48E (TPO/commercial ITC): must begin construction by Jul 4, 2026, placed in service by Dec 31, 2027
  • FEOC MACR: 40% (2026) → 45% (2027) → 60% (2029+)
  • PLR safe harbor: Residential <1.5 MW retain 5% cost safe harbor if construction begins by Jul 5, 2026 — runway through 2030
  • Solar for All: Federal $7B funding HALTED by EPA/Trump. 24 state AGs suing to restore

Tariff Landscape (Updated Mar 2026)

  • Section 201 tariffs EXPIRED Feb 7, 2026 — first time in 8 years without
  • Section 301 (China): 50% on Chinese solar products (doubled Aug 2024)
  • AD/CVD (Feb 25, 2026): 126% tariffs on Indian panels, 86-143% Indonesia, 81% Laos
  • Section 232 polysilicon: Commerce findings due soon. Could impose tariffs on ALL polysilicon-based imports
  • Chinese LFP batteries: ~82% combined duties
  • Module shortages through year-end 2026 — manufacturers sold out (EnergySage H2 2025)
  • Impact: Supply constraints + tariffs favor established TPO providers with locked supply chains

US Solar Manufacturing

  • Module capacity: ~65 GW annually (exceeds 44 GW demand)
  • Cell capacity: only 3.2 GW — critical bottleneck for FEOC compliance
  • US now produces all major supply chain components (polysilicon → modules) for first time since 2013
  • T1 Energy (TX): 2.1 GW cells by EOY 2026, phase 2 adds 3.2 GW
  • First Solar: Fully domestic thin-film (AL, LA, OH, SC) — 14 GW
  • Qcells: Dalton + Cartersville = 8.4 GW, fully integrated silicon value chain
