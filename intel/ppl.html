Pre-Paid Lease (PPL) Competitive Intelligence

Updated April 17, 2026  |  Palmetto LightReach Competitive Intelligence
Bottom line: Pre-Paid Lease category emerging post-25D expiration. 4 players: Propel/Concert/Enphase, HDM Capital, Participate Energy, Thrive Power. All share same IRS risk (48E commercial treatment of residential assets is untested at scale). All expire after Dec 31, 2027 — PLR has 4-year runway vs PPL’s 2-year.

Category Overview

  • Model: Homeowner pays upfront (60–78% depending on provider ITC pass-through), company owns system for 5–6 years, claims 48E commercial ITC + domestic content + energy community bonuses + MACRS, then transfers ownership
  • Ohm Forecast: 230 MW PPL in 2026, ~10% of market by Q4-26
  • Post-ITC tailwind: 25D expired Dec 31, 2025 — PPL claims to be primary mechanism for homeowners to still access ITC economics
  • Common IRS risk: 48E commercial treatment of residential assets is untested at scale. PLR’s TPO lease/PPA is on firmer structural ground.

The 48E Cliff — Existential for PPL

Critical: 48E/45Y expire after Dec 31, 2027. ALL PPL players lose their entire economic model after 2027. PLR’s TPO can safe harbor through 2030 per Aug 15, 2025 Treasury guidance. This is an existential timeline advantage.
  • Jul 4, 2026: Construction must begin (Physical Work Test) for 48E eligibility
  • Projects <1.5 MW (residential) retain 5% cost safe harbor if construction begins by Jul 5, 2026
  • PLR safe harbor inventory locked through 2030
  • PPL has ~2 months to begin construction at scale for post-2027 eligibility

Cash PPL vs Loan PPL — Critical Distinction

Use this framing: Cash PPL has a legitimate use case (homeowner pays cash upfront, gets ownership, no monthly payment). Loan PPL does NOT have a use case vs PLR monthly TPO — the homeowner takes a 20-25 year credit union loan at 8-10% APR. Monthly payment is same or worse than PLR lease. But the loan PPL only comes with a 10-year battery warranty. PLR lease = 25-year battery warranty and replacement.
  • Same monthly payment. One protects the homeowner for 25 years. The other leaves them exposed after year 10.
  • When the battery fails in year 12 or 15, the loan PPL homeowner pays $8K–$15K out of pocket while still making loan payments. The PLR homeowner pays $0.

Where ALL PPL Players Lose to PLR

  • $0 down — PLR requires nothing upfront. PPL requires $12K–$30K+
  • 25-year full coverage including battery replacement at $0
  • Scale: PLR in 31 + PR vs PPL 3–9 states each
  • ABS capital markets: $1.136B cumulative vs zero across all 4 PPL players
  • 4 cross-sell products (storage, generator, water heater, HVAC)
  • Hardware flexibility: PLR supports multiple OEMs. Propel = Enphase-only. Participate = Tesla-only
  • The Cool Down: 40M+ monthly users vs no PPL media platform
  • 90% production guarantee for full 25 years
  • Safe harbor through 2030 vs PPL 2027 cliff
  • Channel quality program: 4 tiers + 6 KPIs vs zero PPL quality framework

Where PPL Wins vs PLR

  • Ownership transfer at year 5–6. PLR never transfers.
  • Zero escalators
  • Zero collections risk (prepaid)
  • Post-ITC narrative: “The ITC isn’t gone — we can still get it for you”
  • Only cheaper for TRUE CASH buyers

PLR Counter-Strategy

  • Lead with $0 down
  • Lead with 25-year all-inclusive (battery replacement, maintenance, monitoring at $0)
  • Lead with the battery warranty gap (10-yr vs 25-yr)
  • Highlight IRS risk on 48E commercial treatment of residential assets
  • Hammer the July 4, 2026 + Dec 31, 2027 cliff — PLR safe harbor through 2030
  • Lead with storage attachment trend — 38% national, HI 92%, CA NEM3 87%. PLR offers battery-only (EB). No PPL player does.
