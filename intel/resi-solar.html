Residential Solar Market Intelligence

Updated April 17, 2026  |  Palmetto LightReach Competitive Intelligence
Bottom line: Residential solar is in a structural transition. 25D expired. TPO takes over. Market shrinks in MW but quality tightens. PLR positioned to benefit from consolidation around higher-credit TPO platforms.

The 2026 Structural Shift

TPO is now the ONLY way homeowners access federal tax credit. 25D eliminated with OBBBA Jul 2025. 48E (commercial/TPO) remains through end of 2027 with safe harbor extension paths. This structural shift benefits established TPO platforms with capital markets access and equipment procurement scale — PLR specifically.
