Loan ABS Market — Deteriorating

  • KBRA Solar Loan ABS Index (Jan 2026): Early-stage AND late-stage delinquencies at ALL-TIME HIGH since index inception. Net losses +173 bps YoY
  • Feb 2026: Late-stage delinquencies up 12 bps to 2.13%. Net losses +122 bps YoY
  • KBRA (Mar 19, 2026): 18 classes downgraded across 18 GoodLeap/LoanPal/Mill City trusts
  • S&P (Sep 30, 2025): 13/16 GoodLeap ratings downgraded
  • Fitch (Jun 2025 + Feb 2026): Class B/C downgrades. “Non-Prime Loans and State Concentrations Drive Defaults”
  • PLR is NOT in this index — TPO lease/PPA is a different asset class

Lease vs Loan — Structural Outperformance

  • KBRA: lease ABS performance 98% vs loan ABS 83.6%
  • 88 loan ABS class downgrades vs 2 lease class downgrades in recent surveillance
  • Structural reasons: homeowner satisfaction higher on TPO (company owns system, handles maintenance), no out-of-pocket battery replacement exposure, better customer service alignment
  • When homeowners feel lied to on loans → stop paying → ABS degrades. Less exposure in lease structures.

  • Combined Sunnova + Mosaic ABS ~$10B in runoff — freeing investor capital for higher-quality issuers like PLR
  • 53+ total rating downgrades on US solar ABS since mid-2024
