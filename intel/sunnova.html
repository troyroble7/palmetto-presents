Sunnova Post-Bankruptcy Intelligence

Updated April 17, 2026  |  Palmetto LightReach Competitive Intelligence
Bottom line: Chapter 11 filed June 9, 2025 ($10.67B debt, $13.5M cash). Sold to Solaris Assets (GoodFinch). SunStrong now servicing 500K+ customers — reports widespread customer nightmare (3-hour holds, unpaid warranty service, broken production credits). Powerful cautionary tale validating PLR’s Solar 3.0 approach.

Detailed Timeline

  • Feb 2025: Cut 15% of workforce for “capital efficiency”
  • Mar 2025: Warned investors they could run out of cash
  • May 28, 2025: Trump DOE revoked $2.92B loan guarantee — death blow
  • Jun 1, 2025: Subsidiary TEP Developer filed Ch.11
  • Jun 5, 2025: 55% workforce layoff (~718 employees)
  • Jun 9, 2025: Sunnova Energy International Ch.11 filed. Case No. 25-90160, S.D. Texas
  • Jul 31, 2025: Sale approved to Solaris Assets LLC (GoodFinch). Credit bid $90M DIP + $25M cash + cure costs
  • Sep 4, 2025: Solaris completed acquisition
  • Nov 12, 2025: Plan confirmed. Nov 14 effective date. Total case: ~155 days
  • Sunnova.com now displays “Sunnova Closure and Support Resources”

SunStrong = New Entity

  • Manages 500K+ solar customers, 4 GW distributed generation + storage
  • Also acquired legacy SunPower assets (2024 bankruptcy)
  • Former Sunnova CEO Paul Mathews = CRO at SunStrong
  • ATLAS negotiating directly with certain dealers for in-process systems

Post-Acquisition Customer Nightmare

The SunStrong transition has been widely reported as a customer service disaster — a cautionary tale in solar ABS history.
  • Production credits no longer honored despite continued billing
  • Old Sunnova portal closed — SunStrong system doesn’t recognize existing accounts
  • 3-hour hold times reported by multiple customers
  • Unauthorized charges, warranty commitments not honored
  • Months without service appointments
  • One customer documented 33.8% production shortfall vs contractual estimates
  • WCVB Boston investigation (Feb 20, 2026) ran the story nationally
  • SunSafe standalone battery lease is DEFUNCT

ABS Implications

  • KBRA placed 28 ratings on Watch Developing across 12 Helios/Hestia transactions
  • One-time delinquency re-aging when SunStrong took over servicing Nov 2025
  • ~$6B in Sunnova ABS now in runoff under new servicer
  • DOE loan disposition: ~$2.9B formally de-obligated back to federal government. $371.6M in Project Hestia DOE-guaranteed bonds NOT included in restructured debt.

Dealer Network Dispersion

  • Pre-BK: ~450 dealers
  • At filing: ~175 active
  • ~275 dealers left pre-bankruptcy (couldn’t get paid)
  • Dispersed to: Sunrun, Palmetto LightReach, GoodLeap, EnFin, Freedom Forever
  • TEPH paid $15M for certain systems + dealer settlement rights

Root Causes of Collapse

  • $10.67B debt vs $13.5M cash at filing
  • Rising interest rates compressed margins
  • California NEM 3.0 killed largest market (CA installs fell 45%)
  • Trump DOE revoked $2.92B loan guarantee (May 28, 2025)
  • Residential solar installs fell 31% in 2024 (six consecutive YoY decline quarters)
  • Couldn’t pay dealers in early 2025 → origination halted

Key Insight

Sunnova’s underlying solar assets performed well — portfolio was “stable, paying interest” during bankruptcy. The failure was overleveraged capital structure + policy shocks, NOT the TPO model itself.
