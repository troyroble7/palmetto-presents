15-Year Lease & Incentive Philosophy

  • Sunrun offers a 15-year solar + battery lease — shorter than PLR’s 25-year
  • Why 15 years? Sunrun avoids battery replacement responsibility; batteries degrade over 10-15 years
  • Higher monthly payments (same system cost over fewer years)
  • Incentive monetization: Sunrun keeps state and local incentives. PLR passes them 100% to the homeowner
  • Illinois battleground: PLR passes IL Adjustable Block Program incentive 100% to homeowner. Sunrun keeps it.
