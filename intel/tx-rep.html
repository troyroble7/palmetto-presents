TX Market Position

  • Texas ~77 MW Q3-25 (#4 residential state per Ohm)
  • ERCOT deregulated market enables REP offerings
  • Battery attachment lower than CA/HI but growing (utility rate pressure + grid reliability concerns)
  • Strong dealer/EPC ecosystem

Competitive Landscape

  • GoodLeap retreating from TX (Feb 2026) due to low collections — wallet share opportunity for PLR
  • Base Power: TX-only battery-as-a-service, $4B valuation, Lennar partnership
  • SOLRITE Energy: First-ever battery-only VPP in TX (Feb 2026). 12¢/kWh rate, $20/mo flat fee. sonnen hardware
  • Reliant/NRG + GoodLeap: GoodGrid VPP partnership (Nov 2025)
  • Sunrun + NRG: TX VPP partnership (Dec 2025) — NRG hedging across providers
  • Freedom Forever: Dealer-driven EPC (Texas one of top 4 states by 2024 volume)

PLR TX Competitive Advantages

  • Integrated solar + battery lease with $4/kWh battery bonus
  • Energy Backup standalone lease — battery-only for retrofit, no PPL competitor offers this
  • Comfort Plan (HVAC lease) — cross-sell product not available from any other TPO
  • 225 bps ABS spread — lowest cost of capital in solar = competitive EPC payouts
  • Erin Suppo RM coverage — experienced TX territory management

TX Risks & Watch-Items

  • Base Power’s Lennar partnership — could lock up builder channel
  • Module tariffs — 126% India + 86-143% Indonesia + 81% Laos impacts TX installer cost structure
  • Utility rate environment — ERCOT volatility affects customer value proposition
  • Sunrun/GoodLeap footprint still present — not a clean-slate market
